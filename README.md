# La Magia Branding

Create a modern, mobile-friendly website for La Magia Barbershop using the attached Instagram screenshot as the main content reference.

Extract and use the following visible business information:

Business name:
La Magia Barbershop

Business type:
Barbershop

Address:
5 East St, Bound Brook, New Jersey 08805

Phone:
(908) 540-2337

Instagram:
@lamagia.barbershop

Availability:
Appointments and walk-ins

Use the screenshot to understand the brand, logo, haircut styles, interior, storefront, visual identity and overall personality.

Do not recreate the Instagram interface. Build a professional standalone barbershop website inspired by the business shown in the screenshot.

Create these sections:

1. Hero section
Use the La Magia Barbershop name, a strong haircut image, and the text:
“Fresh Cuts. Sharp Style. La Magia.”

Include two prominent buttons:
“Book an Appointment”
“Call Now”

2. Services section
Include editable service cards for:
Haircuts
Skin Fades
Low Fades
Designs
Beard Trims
Kids’ Haircuts
Braids and Specialty Styles

Do not invent prices. Use “Contact for pricing” until real prices are provided.

3. Gallery section
Create a responsive image gallery for haircut photos, fades, designs, braids, shop interior and storefront photos.

4. About section
Write a short, professional description explaining that La Magia Barbershop serves Bound Brook, New Jersey and welcomes appointments and walk-ins.

5. Barbers section
Create editable barber profile cards with a photo, name, specialties and booking button. Use placeholders until barber information is provided.

6. Visit Us section
Display:
5 East St, Bound Brook, New Jersey 08805
(908) 540-2337
Instagram: @lamagia.barbershop

Include buttons for:
Get Directions
Call the Shop
View Instagram

7. Contact and booking section
Create a simple form with:
Name
Phone number
Preferred barber
Preferred date
Preferred time
Requested service
Additional notes

Design direction:
Black, white and subtle red accents
Clean urban barbershop aesthetic
Bold typography
High contrast
Premium but welcoming
Smooth scrolling
Mobile-first layout
Subtle animations
Sticky navigation bar
Large clickable phone and booking buttons on mobile

Use editable placeholder images where the screenshot does not provide a clean standalone image.

Do not publish private customer information.
Do not display Instagram follower or following counts.
Do not claim online booking is confirmed unless a booking system is connected.
